## Step 1

Use 4x 16mm M5 screws with 4 M5 sliding bolts to attach the Idler stand and motor stand to the aluminium extrusion. 

## Step 2

Using 8x spacers with 4x V wheels and 4x 40mm M5 screws with nuts, attach the plant top and plant base together to create a plant carriage which can slide along the extrusion. 

## Step 3

Use a 30mm M5 screw to attach the idler wheel to the idler stand. Then use zip ties to secure the motor to the motor stand and use the grub screws to fix the pulley wheel to the motor.

## Step 4

Then, thread the pulley bet through the top of the extrusion and around the pulley and idler wheels and wrap through the hole in the plant base and secure with zip ties.

Finished! The Aqueliotroph frame is assembled.
